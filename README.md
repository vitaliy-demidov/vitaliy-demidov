### 🧠 Engineering Philosophy & Core Focus

I design and ship robust digital products at the intersection of **deterministic domain logic**, **autonomous multi-agent swarms**, and **Telegram Mini Apps**. My core focus is eliminating hallucinations and runtime liabilities through strict policy guards, type-safe contract schemas, and human-in-the-loop approval gates.

- **Deterministic Agent Swarms:** Multi-role coordination (Primary, Radar, Spec, Benchmark, Inspect, Jurist) with strict Policy Guards, verifiable fact provenance, and zero-price hallucination.
- **AI-Powered Telegram Mini Apps:** High-throughput TMA platforms combining voice processing, LLM structuring (Claude API), Hono.js microservices, and reactive mobile interfaces.
- **Transactional & Deal Engines:** Multi-party escrow settlement workflows, state machine validation, and zero-leak content moderation.
- **System Design & Domain Modeling:** Domain-driven route atlases, interactive architectural maps, and verifiable execution traces.
